Transcription dhab ah

Video-ga waxaa loo diraa adeegga backend-ka, oo ka soo saara codka una dira Groq Whisper. Haddii API-gu cilad galo, fariin qalad ayaa la muujinayaa — captions been ah lama abuuro.

Wax ka beddel sahlan

Asturnaanta

Faylka waxaa loo diraa backend-ka transcription-ka. Ha gelin muuqaal xasaasi ah; adeegga server-ka iyo siyaasaddiisa kaydinta ayaa khuseeya.

Caawimo

Sida loo isticmaalo: Dooro video, dooro luqadda ama ogaansho otomaatig ah, riix “SAMEE CAPTIONS-KA”, sax natiijada haddii loo baahdo, kadibna soo dejiso SRT ama VTT.

Haddii upload-ku fashilmo, hubi internet-ka, in video-gu ka yar yahay 100 MB, iyo in cod ku jiro. Codka laga soo saaro video-ga wuxuu leeyahay xad 24 MB oo ku jira backend-ka.
